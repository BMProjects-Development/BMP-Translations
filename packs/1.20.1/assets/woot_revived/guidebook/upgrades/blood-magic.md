# Blood Magic Upgrades

<ItemImage id="bloodmagicwootaddon:blood_collector_upgrade" scale="3"/>

The upgrades from the addon for Blood Magic

## <ItemImage id="bloodmagicwootaddon:blood_collector_upgrade" scale="0.5" /> Blood Collector Upgrade

You first need to bind the upgrade to a <ItemImage id="bloodmagic:altar" scale="0.5"/> Blood Altar by right-clicking on it
before putting it in the factory.

It works like the Well Of Suffering Ritual, it will consume <WootConfig key="addon.bloodmagic.blood_collector_cost" /> life essence 
per generation from your soul network, then generate the mob amount of life essence and store it in the bound Blood Altar.

It will do nothing and will not consume life essence if it is not bound to any <ItemImage id="bloodmagic:altar" scale="0.5"/> Blood Altar.
